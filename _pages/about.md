## About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Software Engineering at École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS) in Montréal, Canada. My research focuses on software measurement, software estimation, and software engineering education.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I accumulated extensive professional experience in software project management and consulting. My research interests are strongly connected to practical challenges in software engineering and project management.

## Research Interests

Software Measurement · Software Estimation · COSMIC Function Points · Early Software Sizing · Software Engineering Education

## Current Research

My doctoral research focuses on software measurement and estimation, with particular interests in early software sizing techniques, COSMIC functional size measurement, size–effort relationships, and educational approaches to software estimation.

## Professional Background

Prior to my doctoral studies, I worked in the software industry in China for approximately ten years, with experience in software project management and consulting.
