## Conference Presentations

### Challenges on Standardization of User Stories and COSMIC Sizing

**IWSM-MENSURA 2024**  
École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), Montréal, Canada  
September 30, 2024

Presentation on the practical challenges of standardizing user stories for software measurement and the application of COSMIC functional size measurement and approximate sizing techniques to user stories.
