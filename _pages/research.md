## Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of software measurement, software estimation, and software engineering education.

- Software Measurement
- Software Estimation
- COSMIC Functional Size Measurement
- Early Software Sizing
- Software Size–Effort Relationships
- Software Engineering Education
- Educational Games for Software Engineering

## Current Research

### Early Software Sizing

My research investigates techniques for estimating software functional size at early stages of the software development lifecycle, when requirements information is still incomplete.

### Software Size and Effort Estimation

I am interested in the relationship between software functional size and development effort, including the construction and evaluation of software estimation models.

### Software Engineering Education

My research also explores the use of educational games to support the teaching and learning of software estimation and measurement concepts.

### COSMIC Functional Size Measurement

COSMIC functional size measurement is a central component of my research and professional experience. My work connects the application of COSMIC in industry with empirical research on software sizing and estimation.
