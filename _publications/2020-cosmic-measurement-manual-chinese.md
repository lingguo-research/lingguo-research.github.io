**Role:** Chinese Translator and Reviewer

Chinese translation of the complete three-part **COSMIC Measurement Manual, Version 5.0**, based on ISO 19761.

The manual consists of:

- **Part 1:** Principles, Definitions & Rules
- **Part 2:** Guidelines
- **Part 3:** Examples

I contributed to the Chinese translation and review of all three parts of the manual.
