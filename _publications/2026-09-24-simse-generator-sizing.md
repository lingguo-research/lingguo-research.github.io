This paper presents a COSMIC functional sizing study of the SimSE educational software environment, including its game generator, shared analyzer tool, and six generated educational games.

The study establishes an empirical functional-size baseline for the SimSE portfolio and supports future research on software effort estimation, evolution planning, and benchmarking.
